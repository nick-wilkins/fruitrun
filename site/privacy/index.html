Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Smoothie Scramble is a game for children made by Nick Wilkins Limited. This page explains what the game does with information. The short version: it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect names, email addresses, locations, photos, contacts, device identifiers or any record of how the game is played.

What stays on your device

The game saves progress on the device it is played on: the names typed for each player, stars, fruit, best times, trophies and the sound setting. A player name is only used to tell players apart on that device and to show who is who when playing together. None of this is sent to us. The only time any of it leaves the device is when playing together, described below. Deleting the game deletes all of it.

Playing together

Players in the same room can race each other, each on their own iPad or iPhone. To do this the game asks permission to find devices on the local network. When a player starts a game, their device announces it under the player's name so that friends can find it. Other devices that are close by or on the same Wi-Fi network and are looking for a game in Smoothie Scramble can see that name. Once friends have joined, the devices send each other the players' names, the level chosen, where each marble is on the track and the finishing times. This goes directly from device to device. It does not pass through the internet or any server of ours, and it is not stored once the game is closed. If you would rather a real name was not shown, use a nickname as the player name.

Purchases

The game is free to play. One optional purchase unlocks races against the computer, cups and starting a game with friends. Joining a game a friend has started is free. The purchase can only be reached after a question meant for a grown-up. It is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, your Apple account or your payment details. Apple tells the game only whether the unlock is owned.

Children

The game is made for children and is built so that no personal information is needed or collected from them. The only links out of the game are behind the same grown-up question.

Other companies

The game contains no code from advertising or analytics companies and shares no information with any third party.

This website

This website sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics. The race you can play on it runs entirely in your browser: it stores nothing, not even your best time, and sends us nothing about how you play. Like any website, the servers that deliver it may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service running and secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@smoothiescramble.com.

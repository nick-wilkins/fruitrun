How we made Smoothie Scramble

Made by a dad, with his kids in charge.

Hi, I'm Nick. I'm a dad, and Smoothie Scramble is a game I made with my children. I did the building. They did the deciding.

It started with smoothies

We like making smoothies at home. I wanted to make something the kids could be part of from the very start, not something handed to them once it was finished. So the game became about the thing we already do together: collect the fruit, tip it into the blender and see what comes out.

The kids were in charge

My children are the play testers. They decided how the game should work, and they chose the names. Every map went in front of them, and they told me exactly what they thought of it.

I wrote the code. They are the reason it plays the way it does.

We made the pictures together

We sat down at the computer together. I showed them how to create an image, and how to ask for changes when it was not quite right. Then we kept refining it until it was.

Why there are no adverts

As a dad, I hated the adverts in children's games. The ones I hated most were the interactive ones: a little game that pops up in the middle of the game I had actually said yes to, and that my kids would start playing. I had allowed one game, not whatever turned up in between.

So Smoothie Scramble has none. Not one advert, no tracking and no accounts. The whole game is free to play on your own, with a single unlock, paid once, for the racing. It needs no internet, so it works in the car.

It is the game I wanted to be able to hand to my own kids without watching over their shoulders. I hope it is that for yours too.

Nick, and the play testers

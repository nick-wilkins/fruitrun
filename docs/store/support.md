**Smoothie Scramble: Help**

**How do I unlock races and cups?**

Tap Race on any level, or Cups or Together on the map, then "Ask a grown-up". After a short sum for the grown-up, tap Unlock. It is one payment. There are no adverts and nothing else to buy.

**I already paid. How do I get races back?**

On the first screen tap "Grown-ups", answer the sum, then tap "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple account that made the purchase. With Family Sharing switched on, one purchase covers the whole family.

**It says it is waiting for a grown-up.**

The device uses Ask to Buy. The family organiser gets a request on their own device. Once they approve it, races open by themselves.

**How do we play together?**

Each player needs their own iPad or iPhone with the game unlocked. Switch Wi-Fi on for every device. You do not need to be connected to a network or the internet.

1. On every device, open the map and tap Together.
2. One player taps "Start a game".
3. The others tap "Join a game" and then tap the game when it appears.
4. The player who started picks a level and presses Start.

Up to four can play.

**My friend's game does not appear.**

- Check Wi-Fi is switched on for both devices.
- Stay in the same room.
- The first time, the game asks to find devices on the local network. If "Don't Allow" was tapped, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Local Network, and switch Smoothie Scramble on.
- Make sure both devices have the latest version of the game.

**How do I remove a player?**

On the first screen tap "Playing as" at the bottom, then the ✕ next to the player's name. Their stars, fruit and best times are removed for good.

**Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?**

Yes. Progress is kept only on the device. The purchase is not lost: use "Restore purchase" after installing again.

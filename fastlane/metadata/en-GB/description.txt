Roll Pip the marble down wild 3D tracks, collect fruit and blend it into smoothies!

Smoothie Scramble is a marble run game made for children aged 6 to 8. Slide one finger to steer. Dodge crates, fly over jumps, choose a way through the forks and hit the speed pads. Finish fast to win stars and fruit, then take your fruit to the blender and see what you can make.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 68 tracks across three lands: Sunny Meadow, Tropical Falls and Blossom Village
• 28 fruits and 58 extras to collect and blend
• A smoothie book that remembers every recipe you find
• 69 trophies to earn
• Up to four players on one device, each with their own stars and fruit
• Roll against the ghost of your own best run

FREE TO PLAY
Every track, every fruit and the blender are free. You can also try a race on the first track, and join a game that a friend has started.

ONE UNLOCK FOR ALL THE RACING
A single purchase, made by a grown-up, opens:
• Races against Pip's friends on every track, at four speeds from Easy to Legendary
• 19 cups: four races in a row, with points for every place
• Starting a game with friends nearby. Up to four can race, each on their own iPhone or iPad, with no internet needed

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• No adverts
• No accounts, no tracking and nothing collected
• One purchase, paid once, behind a question for grown-ups
• Works with Family Sharing
• Plays without an internet connection

Smoothie Scramble is a marble run game for children aged 6 to 8, submitted to the Kids Category. No account or sign-in is needed.

THE IN-APP PURCHASE (races_unlock, non-consumable)
Everything except racing is free. To find the purchase: start a new player, open the map, tap a level, tap Race and choose Medium (Easy on level 1 is a free taster), or tap "Cups" in the top bar. The unlock screen appears. Tap "Ask a grown-up", answer the multiplication sum, then tap Unlock. "Restore purchase" is on the same screen and also on the first screen under "Grown-ups".

PARENTAL GATE
Buying, restoring and the links to the privacy policy and help page are all behind the same gate: a multiplication sum answered on a number pad. There are no other links out of the app.

LOCAL NETWORK PERMISSION
"Together" on the map lets up to four children in the same room race, each on their own device. It uses Bonjour and a direct connection between the devices (service _smoothierace._tcp). Nothing goes through the internet or a server. The permission is asked for only when a child taps "Start a game" or "Join a game", after a screen explaining why. Two devices are needed to try it; the rest of the game does not use the network at all.

DATA
The app collects no data. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Progress is stored only on the device.

MUSIC
The music is licensed from a royalty-free library for use in the app.
